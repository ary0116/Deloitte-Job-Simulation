# Deloitte-Job-Simulation
it is a data analyst job simulation hosted by Forage and sponsored by Deloitte Australia. 
This job simulation focuses on analyzing payroll datasets to identify discrepancies and surgace trends
that informed impoved payroll strategies.

## The Question
In this simulation there are 2 big underlying areas that the client seeks to improve.
1. Which location did machines break the most and what are the machines that broke most often in that location?
2. A worrisome number of internal complaints about gender inequality in terms of salary, find out the unfair salaries.


## The Data
- Source: Task-5-Equality-Table.csv in Raw Data
- Size: [Factory, Job Role, Equality Score, Equality class]

## Process & Tools
| Stage | Tool | What I Did |
|-------|------|-----------|
| Cleaning & exploration | Excel | Removed duplicates, create equality class |
| Visualization | Tableau | Dashboard for campaign insights |

## Key Findings
1. The daikibo factory in the Seiko location have the highest down time, with the Shenzhen location having the second highest
2. The machine that have the highest down time at the Seiko location is Laser Welder
3. Almost 50% of the employee received unfair to highly discriminative pay based on the Equlity Score they've received
